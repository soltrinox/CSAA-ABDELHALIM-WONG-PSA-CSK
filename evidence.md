## Why these materials are the spine

The February 25, 2021 [CSAA denial letter](MERITS-EXHIBITS/CSAA_Denial_Letter-Feb-25-2021.pdf) states that CSAA had "concluded our investigation" after "carefully reviewing the facts and circumstances." The filed Contra Costa complaint, [C26-02801](04-CASE-C26-02801/INDEX.md), treats that sentence as a representation of completed historical acts.

The police report CSAA obtained identified the 911 recording, body-worn camera footage, and computer-aided dispatch records. Those are the materials on this page. The C26-02801 complaint alleges that the claim file recorded no request, receipt, or review of them before the twenty-one-day closure.

The same materials later appear in the trial record:

- January 17, 2025: Motions in Limine [No. 6](MERITS-EXHIBITS/CGC-21-594102-CT1-MIL-No-6_Clerk-pp-149-151.pdf) and [No. 17](MERITS-EXHIBITS/CGC-21-594102-CT1-MIL-No-17_Clerk-pp-238-241.pdf) treat the 911 materials as not produced or of unknown origin.
- February 18, 2025: counsel states the 911 call is "news to me" and that the office did not receive it until the MSC. [Hearing exhibits](04-EXHIBITS/EXHIBIT-Hearing-Feb18-2025.pdf).
- April 16, 2025: counsel states the videos "were provided by Dolan." [Trial Day 7](05-TRANSCRIPTS/REPORTER-TRANSCRIPTS/Trial-Day-07-April-16-2025.pdf).
- April 17, 2025: the driver authenticates the 911 call: "Yes. That is my voice." [Trial Day 8](05-TRANSCRIPTS/REPORTER-TRANSCRIPTS/Trial-Day-08-April-17-2025.pdf).

CSAA's later 802 papers describe a completed investigation and a jury that "agreed." Those writings are linked in the pin register below. The personal-injury verdict decided the insured's negligence. It did not decide what the February 25, 2021 claim file contained.

## 911 call transcripts

### Passerby witness (official transcript)

*Passerby calling from a cell phone in a vehicle. The operator already has a van-hit-scooter report. The witness sees no van and no one helping the injured person.*

| Speaker | Text |
|---------|------|
| **911 Operator** | "San Francisco, 911 exact location of the emergency." |
| **Witness** | "Yeah, hi, Tahoma and this, um, was it Avenue or Street down below Mission?" |
| **911 Operator** | "Uh-huh" |
| **Witness** | "Um, there's a guy that's fallen off of his, um, scooter, the, like, electric foot scooter thing." |
| **911 Operator** | "I have a call saying that there was a van that might have hit a scooter." |
| **Witness** | "There's a van?" |
| **911 Operator** | "A van." |
| **Witness** | "A van or what?" |
| **911 Operator** | "Yeah, uh, I'm getting a call saying that a van might have hit a pedestrian on a scooter that's there." |
| **Witness** | "Oh, oh." |
| **911 Operator** | "Do you see the van there?" |
| **Witness** | "No, there's no van that I could see, but I couldn't tell if anybody was calling or what, but somebody may have called it in. Yeah, the guy's on the ground on the sidewalk." |
| **911 Operator** | "Okay. Hold on one second." |
| **Witness** | "It might be a hit and run. I don't know." |
| **911 Operator** | "Hold on one second here. So he's on the sidewalk by the scooter." |
| **Witness** | "Yeah. He looks like he's in a lot of pain. I'm just calling just to make sure that somebody's called." |
| **911 Operator** | "Yeah, we, it looks like we have police and, uh, medics en route right now. What's your name?" |
| **Witness** | "Okay. Okay. Good. Thanks." |
| **911 Operator** | "Okay." |

### Abdelhalim 911 call (14:53 and 15 seconds)

*The driver identifies himself, admits contact, and the injured person is heard on the same call.*

| Speaker | Text |
|---------|------|
| **911 Operator** | "San Francisco 911, police, fire, or medical?" |
| **Caller** | Yeah, there is, uh, |
| **Caller** | I was, uh, I'm driving a van, and there is a guy just going to cross, |
| **Caller** | He cross from me, and he's barely hitting anything, but I'm calling for him. |
| **Caller** | Uh, we are at Tehama and 5th Street. |
| **Victim** | Oww! |
| **911 Operator** | Okay. You said to Tehama and fifth? |
| **911 Operator** | Yes. Okay. And I'm sorry, can you talk directly into the phone? What happened? |
| **Victim** | **He hit me with his van!** |
| **Caller** | Me was just starting. Uh, he was, he was, I'm turning on to the right and he was **BOOM! I just hit him. HIT HIM.** There was barely anything. I'm calling the 911 for him. |
| **911 Operator** | Oh, okay, so you were in your van, you were driving, and he was on the street, you hit a pedestrian? |
| **Caller** | I was turning to the left side, and he was driving a scooter and he was crossing. |
| **911 Operator** | Oh, on a scooter. Got it. Okay. *[timestamp 14:54 and 53 seconds]* |
| **911 Operator** | Okay, hold on. And is he down on the ground? |
| **Caller** | He is laying down. I don't know. |
| **Victim** | Ahhh, owww! |
| **911 Operator** | Okay. I can, that's him yelling in the background. Okay. *[timestamp 14:55, and 13 seconds]* |
| **911 Operator** | Okay. Hold on, don't hang up yet. |
| **911 Operator** | What, what kind of van are you in? Like a minivan or a cargo van? |
| **911 Operator** | Okay. What's your name, sir? |
| **Caller** | My name is **Subhi. S-U-B-H-I**, okay. |
| **911 Operator** | S-U-V-H-I. Alright, there's gonna be medics on the way with the police, okay? |
| **Caller** | Thank you. |
| **911 Operator** | You're welcome, bye bye. |
