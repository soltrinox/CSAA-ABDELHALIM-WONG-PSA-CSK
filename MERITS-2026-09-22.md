## How the families fit the C26 count

**Driver excerpts (D-01 to D-04).** The 2021 denial treated the insured as not liable. The later deposition denied leaving and denied impact. Trial Day 8 then authenticated the 911 call as the insured's voice. Those clips go to whether a completed, careful review of the report-identified recordings had occurred on February 25, 2021.

**Carrier excerpts (C-01 to C-03).** C-01 is the representation. C-02 is the report order. C-03 is the report that named 911, BWC, and CAD. The C26 complaint alleges the file closed without requesting those named materials.

**Counsel excerpts (P-01 to P-12).** P-06 through P-11 are the 2025 non-receipt and then "provided by Dolan" sequence. P-01 and P-04 are the 2026 restatements that an investigation was performed. P-02, P-03, and P-05 are privilege labels placed on the same 2021 letter. The C26 page treats the later writings as knowledge and ratification evidence, not as a second deceit.

**Court excerpts (J-01 to J-03).** J-01 shows the personal-injury verdict did not try the 2021 file. J-02 and J-03 are the 801 and 802 strike orders now on appeal. They do not adjudicate Contra Costa Case No. C26-02801.
