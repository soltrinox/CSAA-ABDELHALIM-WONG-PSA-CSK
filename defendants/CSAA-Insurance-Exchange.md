# CSAA Insurance Exchange - liability insurer

**Roles and appearances:** Liability insurer; entity alleged to have funded and directed the defense in the underlying trial (CGC-21-594102); named defendant in the equity action (CGC-25-631801); **sole** defendant in the denial-letter/UCL action (CGC-25-631802); **sole** defendant in Contra Costa **C26-02801** (filed common-law deceit).

## Stated position / claims
Plaintiff’s pleadings allege that CSAA, in its February 25, 2021 denial letter, formally denied liability by claiming its investigation showed the insured was not at fault. In litigation, CSAA’s position via its PMK (Nicholas Raffin) and filed anti-SLAPP/demurrer motions is that its claims handling was proper and that plaintiff's lawsuits target protected petitioning activity.

## Rap sheet (conduct alleged in pleadings vs. record)

### Fraudulent Omission and Bad-Faith Denial (UCL - Bus. & Prof. Code 17200)
**Narrative:** Plaintiff's pleadings allege CSAA issued a liability denial letter on February 25, 2021, while intentionally omitting known adverse evidence (such as the 911 call from the driver and the police investigation details). This conduct is alleged to constitute an unfair, unlawful, or fraudulent business practice under the UCL, designed to chill third-party claims before litigation commences.

### Funding and Directing Fraud on the Court (Civil Conspiracy / Aiding & Abetting)
**Narrative:** Plaintiff's pleadings allege that CSAA, as the funding insurer, ratified and directed the litigation tactics of its retained defense counsel (Carbone Smith & Koyama, then Phillips Spallas & Angstadt). This includes the alleged suppression of the "Dolan" admission and the knowing presentation of false narratives to the trial court, constituting extrinsic fraud.

### Common-law deceit about a completed investigation (C26-02801)
**Narrative:** The filed Contra Costa complaint alleges that CSAA's February 25, 2021 letter represented that CSAA had "concluded our investigation" after "carefully reviewing the facts and circumstances," while the claim file recorded no request, receipt, or review of the report-identified 911, body-worn-camera, and CAD materials. The action is pleaded under Civil Code sections 1709 and 1710, subdivision (1). It does not seek private enforcement of Insurance Code section 790.03.
